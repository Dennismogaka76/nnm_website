About Us

An established Kenyan law firm, founded in 2001

Naikuni, Ngaah & Miencha Co. Advocates provides legal services with integrity and practical solutions.

Our history

The firm was established in 2001 by Mr. Lucas Leperes Naikuni and Justice Jairus Ngaah. In 2009, Mr. Isaac Omeke Miencha joined the firm, which then took its current name — Naikuni, Ngaah & Miencha Co. Advocates.

Today the firm describes itself as a boutique practice, handling conveyancing and commercial matters, including transactions and litigation, alongside work in banking, intellectual property, employment, immigration, succession and constitutional law.
