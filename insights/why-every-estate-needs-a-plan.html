Why Every Estate Benefits from a Plan

NNM Advocates · 20 August 2026

Without a plan, families may face uncertainty and delay when a loved one passes away. Estate planning allows a person to set out clear wishes in advance.

What estate planning can involve

  • Preparing a valid will
  • Identifying beneficiaries and executors
  • Organising records of assets
  • Reviewing the plan as circumstances change

Every family's circumstances are different, so tailored legal advice is recommended.
